First Time in College?

Here's everything you need to bring with you.

Things You Need To Bring

ID Proof

Aadhar Card / PAN Card / Passport / Driving Licence

Admission Letter

Original admission letter issued by the college.

Documents

10th, 12th Marksheet, Transfer Certificate, etc.

Photographs

Passport size photographs (4–6 copies).

Fee Receipt

Payment receipt or transaction proof.

Stationery

Notebook, pens, pencils and highlighter.

Laptop

If required for your course.

Personal Essentials

Water bottle, medicines, charger, toiletries etc.

Keep both original and photocopies of all important documents.
